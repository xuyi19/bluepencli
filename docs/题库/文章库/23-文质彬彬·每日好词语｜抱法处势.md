# 文质彬彬·每日好词语｜抱法处势

> 来源：中国青年报 · 2026-08-26 · 正文约 889 字

（2026年08月26日  01版）

坚守法度，凭借权势。这是韩非子提出的法治观念。“法”是所有民众必须共同遵守的法令，规范人们的行为与是非善恶的判定。“势”是统治者的地位和权力。“抱法处势”即要求统治者凭借自己占有和掌握的“势”，以赏罚手段确保法令的执行，从而维护统一的社会秩序与价值。

Upholding Law by Means of Power

This expression means to uphold the law by means of power. This is a way of governance advocated by Hanfeizi. Law is what all people have to abide by, regulating people's conduct as well as the judgment of right or wrong, good or evil. Power refers to the position and strength of the ruler. "Upholding law by means of power" requires the ruler to use his power to ensure that the law is enforced through reward and punishment so as to maintain social order and social values.

中者，上不及尧、舜而下亦不为桀、纣，抱法处势则治，背法去势则乱。（《韩非子·难势》）（平庸的统治者，上不能比肩尧、舜，下不至沦为桀、纣，若坚守法度、凭借权势，则能实现政治安定，若背离法度、放弃权势，则会导致政治混乱。）

An ordinary ruler, not as good as Yao or Shun, yet not descending to the ways of Jie or Zhou, upholds the law by means of power to realize social stability. If he turns his back on the law and abnegates his power, society will fall into disorder. (Hanfeizi)
